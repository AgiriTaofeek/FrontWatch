# Operational Risks

## R01 — FrontWatch Cannot Observe Itself

Mitigation: independent platform metrics, dashboards, alerts.

## R02 — Alert Storm

Mitigation: grouping, deduplication, actionable thresholds.

## R03 — Silent Data Staleness

Mitigation: freshness SLIs/SLOs.

## R04 — Capacity Exhaustion

Mitigation: forecasting and early warnings.

## R05 — Slow Incident Response

Mitigation: runbooks, on-call, clear ownership.

## R06 — Deployment Regression

Mitigation: deployment markers and post-deploy health comparison.

## R07 — Monitoring Dependency Failure

Mitigation: layered health checks and independent infrastructure monitoring.
