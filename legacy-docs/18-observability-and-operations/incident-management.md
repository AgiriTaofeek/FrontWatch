# Incident Management

## Lifecycle

```text
Detection
 ↓
Triage
 ↓
Severity
 ↓
Incident owner
 ↓
Mitigation
 ↓
Root-cause investigation
 ↓
Recovery
 ↓
Postmortem
```

## Roles

For larger incidents:

```text
Incident Commander
Technical Lead
Communications
Scribe
```

One person may fill multiple roles in a small team.

## Communication

Record:

```text
what happened
impact
current mitigation
next update
```

## Incident Timeline

Use timestamps for major events.

## Closure

Do not close an incident merely because symptoms disappeared.

Capture follow-up work.
